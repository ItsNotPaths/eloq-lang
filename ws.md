## Workstream D: build the decided design

The decisions are in `docs/design.md`. In short:

- `pending-count`: no `count()` or `capacity()` calls. Tables get `.count` (rows at step
  start) and `.added` (pending adds); capacity is a plain constant.
- `abcont`, `abend-removed-flags`: two kinds of code. An abend stops the step and clears
  pending flags. An abcont skips the failed row and finishes the step. `TABLE_FULL` and
  index out of range are abconts. Undoing a step is the host's own copy.
- `abend-detail`: one fixed `Abend_Detail` in the state: line, row, value, abcont_count.
- `coercion`: automatic both ways by C rules, no casts; `--coercions` lists each one.
- `index-syntax`: row order, `enemies[target].position`, reads and writes. An index write
  counts as writing the whole column.
- `whole-array-procs`: no body loops; a foreign proc on a table gets `Table *` once.
- `opaque-strings`: built-in `string` as Odin's `{data, len}`; fixed text is `[N]char`.
- `proc-overloading`, `vector2-procs`: procs share a name when input slot types differ.

Build `abcont` first: `abend-detail` and `index-syntax` wait on it.
